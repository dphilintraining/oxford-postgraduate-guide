## Lincoln College scholarships versus grants (checked 27 November 2026)

Lincoln College lists three distinct awards. The automatically considered Kingsgate Graduate Scholarships cover fees and a stipend for incoming DPhil applicants. The college page mentions up to ten awards for 2027-28, but marks the final number TBC. No separate scholarship application is required. Kingsgate Graduate Grants are separate £20,000 awards (twelve listed) with a 1 June 2027 college application form. The Crewe Graduate Awards are separate £5,000 awards (ten listed), also requiring a form by 1 June 2027. All are linked to the [official Lincoln College funding page](https://lincoln.ox.ac.uk/study-here/graduate-study/finance-and-funding/).

The unverified YouTube example link and the empty grey founder-story placeholder were removed from the public Apply page. The visible request for further contributor examples remains.
