The story behind the guide.

A student-led project created to make Oxford postgraduate applications less opaque, and to help more people ask the right questions.

SR
Meet the founder

Hello, I'm Sini.

I'm Sinibaldo Romero Arocha, an MD/PhD student at the University of Minnesota and a DPhil student at the University of Oxford. My research at Oxford's Kennedy Institute of Rheumatology sits at the intersection of immunology, metabolism, and inflammatory disease.

My route to Oxford was through the NIH Oxford–Cambridge Scholars Program, a specialised pathway that differs from the standard Oxford DPhil application process. That experience has made me interested in how differently postgraduate education and research training can work across institutions.

Why this project exists

University websites are essential, but they're not always written from the perspective of someone encountering the system for the first time. I wanted to create a welcoming, independent place that brings together official resources, practical organisation tools, and honest student experiences.

Our goal is not to promise admission or to suggest there is only one way to succeed. It is to give prospective students more clarity and confidence as they explore their options.

Our editorial approach

This is an unofficial, independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the University of Oxford. Admission, funding, and college requirements can change. Use Oxford’s official Graduate Application Guide, your course page, and the relevant scholarship administrators for authoritative guidance.

Where we discuss my experience, we label it as personal perspective rather than universal advice.

Get in touch

Have a question about the guide, spotted outdated information, or want to contribute a student perspective? Get in touch at dphilintraining@gmail.com.

For questions about your own application or eligibility, please contact the relevant Oxford course or scholarship administrator for official guidance.
