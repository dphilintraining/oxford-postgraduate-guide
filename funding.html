Before you apply

Three things to know about Oxford funding.

01

Course deadlines matter

For many Oxford awards, a complete course application by the relevant December or January funding deadline is sufficient for consideration.

02

Some awards need extra steps

Specific scholarships require a statement, an additional form or a separate external application. Look for the application-route label.

03

Studentships vary by project

UKRI and departmental awards often depend on the doctoral project or training programme. Funding does not always cover the full overseas fee difference.

Questions applicants ask

Funding FAQ

Do I need a separate scholarship application?

For many Oxford graduate scholarships, including Clarendon, a complete course application submitted by the relevant December or January funding deadline is enough for automatic scholarship consideration. Others, including many external scholarships, require separate applications or additional statements. The application route shown in the directory is a starting point, not a substitute for the provider's current requirements.

What if I miss the December deadline?

Check the exact deadline on your chosen course page. Some courses use January funding deadlines, while others have December deadlines or project-specific funding dates. If you miss the relevant funding deadline, you may still be able to apply for admission if the course is open, but you may not be considered for some awards. External funding deadlines can be entirely separate.

Does Clarendon cover international fees?

Yes. Oxford states that Clarendon covers course fees in full for both Home and Overseas fee-status scholars. Full-time recipients also receive a living-cost grant. Check the duration and other terms on the official Clarendon applicant page.
